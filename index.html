finance-assistant

finance-assistant is a personal tool built and used by one person to keep track of their own credit card statements.

It reads the owner's own Gmail with read-only access to find credit card statement emails, extracts the due date, total, and transactions, and saves them to the owner's own Google Sheet. It then sends the owner payment reminders and spending summaries by Telegram.

It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.
