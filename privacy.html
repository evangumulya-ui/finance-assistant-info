Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

finance-assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it has read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account (the gmail.readonly permission). It uses this only to find credit card statement emails from specific banks and to download their PDF attachments. It cannot send, change, or delete any email.

How the data is used

Statement text is processed to extract the due date, amounts, and transactions. Before any text is sent to an AI service for reading, card and account numbers are masked. The results are saved to the owner's own Google Sheet and used to send the owner reminders and summaries by Telegram.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. The only outside service that receives statement text is the AI service used to read it (Anthropic), and only in masked form.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in the owner's own Google Sheet and on the owner's own computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the data from their own Sheet.
